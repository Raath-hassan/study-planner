    Effective Study Techniques

    The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method where you break study time into 25-minute concentrated intervals separated by short breaks. This prevents mental fatigue and maintains deep focus.


    Active Recall involves testing yourself on material rather than passively re-reading it. Try explaining a complex topic in your own words or completing flashcards to solidify your memory retention.


    Spaced Repetition helps transfer knowledge into long-term memory. Review your study materials at expanding time intervals—such as 1 day later, 3 days later, and then a week later.


    About This Application

    All data created within this tool is safely stored inside your web browser using local storage, ensuring that your tasks persist even after you refresh or close the browser window.
